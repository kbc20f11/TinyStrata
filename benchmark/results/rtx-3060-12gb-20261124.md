# GeForce RTX 3060 12 GB benchmark

Measured 2026-11-24 on Windows with an RTX 3060 12 GB (driver 610.88), Ryzen 9 7945HX3D, and CUDA 13.2.86. Built TinyStrata from [ffd5f37](https://github.com/kbc20f11/TinyStrata/commit/ffd5f37cf2017a2f6ce8f1b3951a111d433acedb) for sm_86.

All 16 starts completed. Standard v1 uses INT8 KV, automatic context and Expert placement, and GPU Vision for Qwen and Gemma. Clover uses causal D0 with Vision off.
