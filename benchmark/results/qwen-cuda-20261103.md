### Hybrid with nominal 12 GB

For the CUDA Qwen hybrid route, correct the automatic stage-loan budget by charging the known GPU-side CPU-result buffers (`Tmax * top_k * (embedding_width + 1) * 4`, 67,141,632 bytes here) and 64 MiB of lazy CUDA headroom before Expert placement. The previous budget omitted these costs: both control starts lose their full-cache restoration during warmup and retain 3,494 Expert slots. The corrected budget starts with 4,059 slots and restores every loan successfully.

**Budget-fix ABBA, before the short-prompt threshold:** A is the uncorrected budget; B is the corrected budget with native staging still used for every prompt above the existing hybrid threshold. This is a correctness and capacity-recovery comparison, rather than a fixed-Expert-capacity kernel benchmark.
