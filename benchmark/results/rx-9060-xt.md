### What changed, and what was measured separately

On HIP, Clover INT8 Attention with a 256-element head and GQA8 can share KV reads/dequantization across **two to four adjacent queries**, from **4,096 tokens of history**. Two query rows use a two-query tile; three or four use a four-query tile. Full 256-key chunks use a common path without per-query boundary tests inside the QK/PV loops; the tail retains per-query masks. The ordered FP32 dot products, max/sum reduction tree, 256-key split boundaries and final merge are preserved. Preview still sees the complete noisy block; causal verification keeps its individual row limits. The change does not skip old history or replace exact acceptance with a probability threshold. CUDA, other model families, FP8 and one-query causal decode retain their existing paths.

A separate host-fenced diagnostic localized the long-input increase to Attention work: after 64K input, the recorded Attention-core scope was **16.315 ms per emitted token** for matched causal and **30.632 ms** for old D3. State commit/maintenance was **0.294 ms/token** for D3. Fences and dispatch affect these measurements; they are attribution diagnostics, not the throughput table above. Nested scopes must not be added together. This does not establish a particular hardware-counter or memory-bandwidth cause.

Negative HIP event durations were observed in short diagnostic scopes on this PC, consistent with the [Windows HIP timing issue reported to AMD](https://github.com/ROCm/TheRock/issues/9383). ### Workload-dependent choice

- **Standard comparison:** `standard-int8-d0` retains the common v1 INT8/automatic profile. `stock-fp8-d0` is the matching FP8 option with automatic workers/chunk.
- **Causal tuning for this PC:** `tuned-fp8-d0-w9-c1024` reaches **52.12 tok/s** for short generation and **46.82 tok/s** after 16K, versus **47.58 / 41.90 tok/s** for the automatic FP8 baseline: **+9.5% / +11.7%**. Its 4K/16K input rates are **777.54 / 709.39 tok/s**, about **1.6% lower** than that baseline. It uses no tuning environment variable.
- **Short-input generation:** `tuned-int8-d4-w9-c1024` is fastest here at **59.06 tok/s**, **24.1% above the automatic FP8 baseline** (23.6% above standard INT8). However, after 16K it falls to **28.21 tok/s**, **32.7% below automatic FP8** and **26.5% below standard INT8**. It is a short-input option, not a general speed winner. Its `STRATA_TSUM=1` override and larger Expert cache are part of the measured configuration.
- D3 and the other D4 chunk sizes also improve short generation but remain slower for generation after 16K than the causal profiles. The shared self-speculation default therefore stayed **D0 at that time**. The explicitly selected local tuning does not change the other model profiles or impose those worker/chunk choices on other PCs.
